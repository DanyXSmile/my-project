How to Play

Test your art intuition and history knowledge across 10 exercises!

1. Identify the Original

In every exercise, you are shown 4 images of famous artworks. 3 are modified fakes, and only 1 is the authentic original masterpiece. Inspect them carefully before choosing!

2. Select Difficulty & Earn Points

  • Easy Mode (100 Pts Max): Guess only the authentic image (10 pts per exercise).
  • Medium Mode (100 Pts Max): Guess the authentic image (5 pts) + identify the correct artist (5 pts).
  • Hard Mode (100 Pts Max): Guess the authentic image (3.33 pts) + artist (3.33 pts) + century created (3.33 pts).

3. Review & Discover

After completing all 10 exercises, submit your answers to see a side-by-side comparison of your choices versus the original masterpieces, along with detailed art history backstories!
